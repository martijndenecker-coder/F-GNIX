Syndicate Governance · 25h & 50h Group Blocks

Group Rental Rules & Joint Liability

Individual Sector PIC Logging

Only one pilot may act as Pilot-in-Command (PIC) and log flight time per sector. Group members rotate PIC across multi-leg trips under EASA cost-sharing rules (Reg. (EU) No 965/2012 Art. 6(4a)); dual PIC logging is prohibited.

Individual Checkout Requirement

Signing a group master contract does not confer automatic checkout. Every individual member (3–4 pilots) must complete their own AA-5 checkout with MikeDelta prior to flying as PIC.

Joint and Several Liability (Hoofdelijke Aansprakelijkheid)

All syndicate members share joint and several liability for master account balances, unpaid landing/fuel bills, and the €1,500 hull insurance deductible. The PIC on an incident sector is primarily liable; if unsettled within 14 days, remaining members are jointly liable and group bookings pause until settled.

Contract Terms · Risk & Fuel Protection

Operational Covenants

365-Day Block Validity (“Use-It-or-Lose-It”)

Prepaid flight blocks (10h, 25h, 50h) expire 365 calendar days from purchase with zero refund or cash-out value. Unused hours may be transferred to another vetted roster pilot for a €50 administrative fee.

Fuel Price Benchmark (€2.25/L Excl. VAT)

All wet rates are pegged to AVGAS 100LL at EBKT at or below €2.25/L excl. VAT (€2.72/L incl. 21% VAT). Fuel price spikes above this benchmark are surcharged strictly on actual fuel burned (~40 L/hr).

Safety-First VFR Weather Force Majeure

No-show penalties (1.0h for non-weather cancellations under 24h notice) and daily minimums are waived or rescheduled without penalty when VFR minimums at EBKT or along route prevent safe flight.
